Who we are

Building enduring value across Africa and global markets

Fraser Legacy Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a South African holding company focused on responsible trade, strategic investment and sustainable commercial growth. We work with businesses, investors and specialist partners to identify opportunities, build capable relationships and support long-term value creation.

Our work is guided by a straightforward belief: meaningful growth is created through disciplined planning, sound governance, practical execution and respect for the people and communities connected to our operations.

A long-term approach to opportunity

Markets change, industries evolve and successful businesses must be able to adapt. Fraser Legacy Holdings approaches each opportunity with a long-term perspective, considering commercial viability alongside operational resilience, responsible conduct and the potential for shared value.

We seek to support enterprises and initiatives that strengthen supply chains, enable trade, encourage local participation and contribute to sustainable economic activity.
